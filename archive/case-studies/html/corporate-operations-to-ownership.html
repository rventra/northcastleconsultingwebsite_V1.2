CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT

From Operations to Ownership

Enabling car rental executives to acquire a franchise of a major global brand.

Industry: Travel & Car Rental
Duration: 6 months
Team: 1 Managing Director, 1 Vice President
Services: Corporate Development, Investor Relations

1The Challenge

An independent sponsor team of car rental executives had deep operational expertise and a vision to acquire a franchise. However, they lacked:

  • • Dedicated deal resources
  • • Financial modeling expertise
  • • Capital structuring experience
  • • Investor outreach capabilities

2Our Solution

We partnered to lead critical aspects of the transaction:

  • • Valuation & financial modeling
  • • Capital structuring & investor outreach
  • • Due diligence coordination
  • • Transaction execution support

The Execution

To support the independent sponsor team, we developed key financial tools and structured a financing approach:

○ Built a Detailed Bottom-Up Financial Model

Developed a granular model segmented by location, business line (insurance, maintenance), and key KPIs (such as revenue per car, revenue per transaction, and cost per transaction). This allowed the operations team to evaluate the business through their operational framework and assess where their strategic vision could enhance value.

○ Developed a BI Dashboard for Data-Driven Decision-Making

Created an interactive business intelligence (BI) dashboard that provided insights the independent sponsor team hadn't planned for. This helped the management team effectively communicate their value-creation strategy to investors.

○ Structured a Tax-Advantaged Financing Strategy

Identified a tax exchange opportunity that enabled the business to refinance its car fleet at a higher loan-to-value (LTV) ratio. This significantly reduced the equity capital required from investors. We also assisted in negotiating financing terms with alternative fleet lenders, further optimizing the capital structure.

○ Enhanced Investor Confidence & Negotiated Terms

The granular insights from our financial model and BI dashboard allowed the investor to better understand the business than the existing management. Armed with this data, they successfully convinced investors of the superior returns their operational strategies could generate and secured favorable investment terms.

The Results

The operations team secured multiple investors for both fleet financing and equity investment, allowing them to submit a credible and competitive bid for the franchise. The financial model and KPI dashboards developed were transitioned into the operational foundation for managing the business post-acquisition. By minimizing the required equity investment and showcasing strong upside potential, the sponsor team positioned themselves to generate significant long-term value as operators.
