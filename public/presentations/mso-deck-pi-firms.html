The Docket Leak & the Data Gap

Where the fee leaks — and why funders can't underwrite it

Docket Compensability

Where the Fee Leaks

Between signature and settlement, dockets lose cases to unreturned records, missed deficiency windows, unreachable claimants, incomplete Plaintiff Fact Sheets, and awards scored a tier below what the medicine supports.

  • Intake QA stops non-qualifying cases before they consume records budget — and when retainers are purchased, enforces the vendor's replacement obligation so you only pay for Qualified Signed CFAs.
  • Medical record review and medical curing — retrieve the records, review the diagnosis and treatment history against docket criteria, and close the gaps the matrix will score on.
  • Curing and clarifying documentation — rapidly procure additional documentation such as Plaintiff Fact Sheets and affidavits inside every deficiency window; the administrator scores what it is given.
  • Claimant comms bridge and claimant enrichment — monthly case-update letters that solicit contact-info changes; for unreachable claimants, data enrichment to find current contact information so the docket stays enrolled through a multi-year program.
  • Matrix valuation and tier upgrades move cases from dropped to paid and from tier 3 to tier 2.

ER 1.5(e) · Requires ABS

Beacon Law — our Arizona firm, licensed as an ABS so its nonlawyer owners can hold equity — originates and works up the case, then divides the fee with your firm per case under ER 1.5(e): in proportion to services or with joint responsibility, and with the client’s written consent. Probate Strategy

Outsourced

In cancer dockets like Hair Relaxer and AFFF, claimant mortality before funding is high. Probate is a certainty, not an edge case.

  • Not handled in-house. Multi-state probate run internally destroys operational leverage.
  • Local probate counsel engaged in the claimant's home county per matter, routed through the settlement administrator.
  • Counsel opens the estate, appoints a representative, and executes the distribution; costs come from the client's share.

Appendix F

What Counts as Open — and How a Silent Case Closes

Every Bill Comes With a Status Report

What Counts as an Open Case

Definition
  • Open from the day the case enters our system until it is (1) settled and paid, (2) dropped by the firm in writing, or (3) closed under the no-response process.
  • Any day counts as the month. A case open on any day of a month is billed for that month.
  • Counted per MDL. Each mass tort has its own count and its own tiers.
  • The firm decides. We never drop a client; the firm signs off on every close.

No-Response Close Clock

90 Days
Day 0
Request goes out — for example, an affidavit or a records release.
Day 60
No answer → IAL sent. Immediate Action Letter on firm letterhead, firm-approved wording. Claimant has 30 days.
Day 90
Still no answer → ready to close. Case goes on the monthly close list; the firm approves in one email; billing stops.

Every try to reach the claimant — call, text, email, mail — is logged. If the case is already filed, the attorney takes the court step to end it.
