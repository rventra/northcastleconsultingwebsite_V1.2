SCHEMA SUMMARY · 6 TABLES, 4 LAYERS
Level 1 — gps_pings (720,970 rows): raw GPS samples; source of truth for location, speed, status, odometer.
Level 2 — trips (61,346 rows): one row per delivery; derived from Movement Started → Movement Started boundaries.
Level 3 — daily_summary (2,001 rows): one row per vehicle per active day; trips, miles, active/idle hours, utilization.
Level 4 — hourly_pattern (144 cells): fleet drive hours by weekday × hour for heatmaps.
Static — trucks (8 rows) & depot (1 row): vehicle metadata and home base.
