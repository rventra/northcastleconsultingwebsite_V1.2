FLEET TAKEAWAY
The fleet logs 47% total utilization of weekday available hours, but only 17% gross utilization when measured as drive time. That 30-point gap is idle and transition time. T444 is the clear workhorse at 21% gross / 62% total utilization, while T447 sits at 10% gross / 31% total. A buyer will underwrite to active-hour productivity, not total hours logged.

ACTIVITY TAKEAWAY
Day-shift trucks create a broad mid-day plateau (08:00–16:00), while the night-shift truck (T446) operates 17:00–05:00 with its peak at 11 PM. Weekend activity exists on only 2 of 8 trucks (T441 and T447). The bigger operational question is the 64% idle/transition share: improving depot turnaround or route density would lift effective utilization faster than adding trucks.

SCHEMA SUMMARY · 6 TABLES, 4 LAYERS
Level 1 — gps_pings (720,970 rows): raw GPS samples; source of truth for location, speed, status, odometer.
Level 2 — trips (61,346 rows): one row per delivery; derived from Movement Started → Movement Started boundaries.
Level 3 — daily_summary (2,001 rows): one row per vehicle per active day; trips, miles, active/idle hours, utilization.
Level 4 — hourly_pattern (144 cells): fleet drive hours by weekday × hour for heatmaps.
Static — trucks (8 rows) & depot (1 row): vehicle metadata and home base.
