COHORT GROWTH ENGINE · WHERE THE ARR GROWTH IS COMING FROM
Contribution to ARR growth (pp of opening ARR) — expansion · new-logo · attrition drag
Expansion is upsell into the existing base; new-logo is ARR from logos signed in the period; attrition drag is contraction plus gross churn (negative). Components sum to reported ARR growth.
COHORT-ENGINE TAKEAWAY
Blended growth reads healthy, but it is carried by one segment. Enterprise NDR has climbed 110% → 117% while SMB has slid 95% → 81% and turned net-negative in the trailing period. Filter this page to SMB and the growth engine inverts: attrition outruns expansion and new logos combined. A buyer's first cohort cut will find this; the question is whether the sell side finds it first.
