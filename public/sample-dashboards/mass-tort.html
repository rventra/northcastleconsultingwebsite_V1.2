Tier Criteria — gates first, then the grid

Tier 0 — Eligibility gates (pass/fail, before any tiering)
  • Product/exposure identification — verified records, corroborated claim, or exposure records
  • Exposure era → defendant — pre-1990-only = Chevron (fails for Syngenta MDL)
  • Minimum dose — ≥1 documented season
  • Latency sanity — <5 yrs exposure → dx = likely DQ (prodromal clock excepted)
  • PFS / AQ complete (CMO 7/10)
  • Plausibility screen (CMO 18) — assumed, not modeled
Market value grid — computed from doc score, confounder score, latency, severity, age
  • Tier I $400K–$1M+ — doc ≥7 · latency 10–30 · H&Y ≥3 · confounder 0 · severity escalator (PDD/DBS/nursing home/falls) · dx age <70
  • Tier II $150K–$300K — doc 4–6 · latency 5–35 · confounder ≤1
  • Tier III $20K–$150K — doc <4 · latency <10 · confounder ≥2 · dx age >78 · or drift-only
  • Doc score +2 license / W-2 ag employer / purchase records / co-worker dec · +1 spray-log proximity / brand recall
  • Confounders +1 family hx · LRRK2/GBA+ · TBI w/ LOC · age >78 · maneb/rotenone
Tier 1 PD with dementia (PDD)
  • Confirmed idiopathic PD (neurologist dx)
  • Documented cognitive decline / dementia DSM-5 / MDS
  • Clears all gates PDD protections attach
Tier 2 Confirmed idiopathic PD
  • Movement-disorder neurologist PD diagnosis
  • DaTscan / levodopa response strengthens, not required
  • Clears all gates core inventory
Tier 3 Parkinsonism (non-PD)
  • Parkinsonism NOS / atypical / suspected toxicant-induced
  • Same exposure proof as Tier 2
  • Upgrades to T2 on confirmed PD dx
Tier 4 Prodromal / symptom-level
  • No formal dx; prodromal symptoms documented (tremor, RBD, hyposmia)
  • Contemporaneous medical record — not retrospective self-report
  • Migrates up on confirmed dx without re-filing
Below grid Not eligible
  • Fails an eligibility gate (or fits no tier)
  • Gate cures (PFS, exposure proof) enter the grid at their natural tier
Per docs/paraquat-tier-framework.md: tier placement is driven by diagnosis + exposure proof only — economic loss (wages, medical bills) never sets the tier. Deceased claimants convert to wrongful-death/survival posture; the tier is set by records as of death and never diminishes. Progression migration: PD → PDD moves up automatically on documented cognitive decline; prodromal moves up on confirmed dx. PPE is bidirectional — documented use scores up (rebuts comparative fault); non-use is neutral, never a penalty. Family history and confounders (other pesticides, TBI, smoking) adjust the within-tier score only — they can never drop a claimant a full tier. Latency scores strongest at 10–40 yrs from first exposure to first documented symptom.

Legal developments

  • SCOTUS, Monsanto v. Durnell (Jun 2026): FIFRA preempts state failure-to-warn claims requiring warnings beyond EPA-approved labels. Impact: high — materially strengthens Syngenta's preemption motion in MDL 3004 and its leverage over non-settling inventory.
  • Wells Daubert exclusion, MDL 3004 Doc. 5237 (Apr 2024): plaintiffs' sole general-causation expert was excluded and the first bellwether pool collapsed. Impact: high — general causation is still unproven across all 2,000+ member cases.
  • Roundup $7.25B class settlement (Feb 2026): covers current and future claims; final-approval hearing held Jul 2026, outcome pending. Impact: moderate — the clearest comparator pricing signal for paraquat settlement value.
  • Philadelphia CCP bellwethers: Mertens, the first paraquat jury trial, settled on the eve of trial; 1,952 cases remain pending. Impact: moderate — a plaintiff verdict there would re-price the opt-out tail.
  • Opt-out firm inventory: the MSA excludes clients of specific opt-out firms. Impact: moderate — watch how that inventory is priced, tried, or folded back in.
  • Syngenta ends global paraquat production (Jun 2026): exit proceeds while advocates press EPA for a ban. Impact: low–moderate — shapes future-claims exposure and the public narrative more than current valuation.
