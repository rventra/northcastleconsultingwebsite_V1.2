Tier Criteria — what's required to land in each tier

Tier 1 Confirmed PD + Verified Evidence
  • Parkinson's Disease diagnosis (idiopathic PD)
  • Certified applicator license OR verified purchase receipts
  • ≥10 years of exposure
  • Latency 10–25 years causation sweet spot
  • Age at first exposure < 45 longer life impact
  • Levodopa response (robust or moderate)
Tier 2 Probable PD + Documented Exposure
  • Any PD-spectrum diagnosis (incl. parkinsonism)
  • Documented exposure (claimed or verified)
  • ≥5 years of exposure
Tier 3 Symptoms Only / Partial Evidence
  • Symptoms logged
  • Some exposure evidence (claimed)
  • At least 1 year exposure (or unknown)
Tier 4 Marginal / Below Threshold
  • Fails to meet Tier 3 criteria
Age at first exposure is calculated as (first exposure date) − (date of birth). Medical record count is informational only — it does not gate tier membership. Family history is a scoring factor (10%) with reversed direction: a documented first-degree relative with Parkinsonism dilutes the claim (competing-cause argument — "PD was genetic, not paraquat-caused"); absence of family history is stronger (pure paraquat causation, no competing cause). PPE use (gloves, respirator, coveralls) is a scoring factor (5%): documented PPE use corroborates the professional-applicator pattern and strengthens the case; cases without PPE on file score lower.

Legal developments

  • SCOTUS, Monsanto v. Durnell (Jun 2026): FIFRA preempts state failure-to-warn claims requiring warnings beyond EPA-approved labels. Impact: high — materially strengthens Syngenta's preemption motion in MDL 3004 and its leverage over non-settling inventory.
  • Wells Daubert exclusion, MDL 3004 Doc. 5237 (Apr 2024): plaintiffs' sole general-causation expert was excluded and the first bellwether pool collapsed. Impact: high — general causation is still unproven across all 2,000+ member cases.
  • Roundup $7.25B class settlement (Feb 2026): covers current and future claims; final-approval hearing held Jul 2026, outcome pending. Impact: moderate — the clearest comparator pricing signal for paraquat settlement value.
  • Philadelphia CCP bellwethers: Mertens, the first paraquat jury trial, settled on the eve of trial; 1,952 cases remain pending. Impact: moderate — a plaintiff verdict there would re-price the opt-out tail.
  • Opt-out firm inventory: the MSA excludes clients of specific opt-out firms. Impact: moderate — watch how that inventory is priced, tried, or folded back in.
  • Syngenta ends global paraquat production (Jun 2026): exit proceeds while advocates press EPA for a ban. Impact: low–moderate — shapes future-claims exposure and the public narrative more than current valuation.
