Key Rulings Timeline

  • 2023-09/10Durnell v. Monsanto trial, Circuit Court of the City of St. Louis, MO — $1.25M strict-liability failure-to-warn verdict.
  • 2023-11 → 2025-09-30Anderson/Draeger/Gunther, Cole Cty. MO — $1.56B verdict reduced to $611M and affirmed; Missouri Supreme Court declined review 2025-09-30.
  • 2024-01/06McKivison, Philadelphia — $2.25B verdict reduced to ~$400M.
  • 2024-04-17MDL 3004 Doc. 5237 — Wells Daubert exclusion. Judge Rosenstengel excluded Dr. Martin Wells, plaintiffs' sole general-causation expert, after a 4-day Daubert hearing; the first bellwether pool collapsed days later and a new pool had to be ordered (Jan 2025).
  • 2025-03-21Barnes, Cobb Cty. GA — $2.1B verdict ($65M compensatory + $2B punitive).
  • 2025-05Caranci, Pa. Super. Ct. — $177M verdict stands.
  • 2025-09-29MDL 3004 Doc. 5719 — stay extended; October 2025 bellwether trial vacated.
  • 2025-11Dennis, Cal. Ct. App. — $28M verdict upheld.
  • 2026-01-27/29Mertens, Philadelphia CCP — first-ever paraquat jury trial ended in a confidential eve-of-trial settlement.
  • 2026-06-25Monsanto v. Durnell, SCOTUS No. 24-1068 — 7–2 for Bayer: FIFRA preempts state failure-to-warn claims requiring warnings beyond EPA-approved labels. Industry-changing for both Roundup and paraquat — directly strengthens Syngenta's pending preemption motion in MDL 3004.

Comparator: Roundup (MDL 2741)

  • ~3,920 pending in MDL 2741 (N.D. Cal.) as of Jul 2026; ~61,000 active total; ~100,000 claims resolved for ~$11B. Judge Chhabria issued a Suggestion of Remand 2026-01-05.
  • $7.25B class settlement (current + future claims) announced 2026-02-17; preliminary approval 2026-03-04 (Judge Timothy Boyer, St. Louis 22nd Cir.); objectors' federal removal remanded 2026-06-17. Final-approval hearing held 2026-07-09 — outcome pending not verified
  • Bayer has ~$16B provisioned in total reserves; Durnell (above) is a SCOTUS preemption win that narrows future failure-to-warn exposure.
  • Implication for paraquat: Syngenta filed a preemption MSJ in Feb 2026 — the Durnell holding materially improves its odds and its leverage over non-settling inventory.

What a PE Reviewer Should Watch

  • Settlement-administration milestones — QSF claims-submission rates, lien-administrator throughput, and timing of first disbursements under the MSA; these set the real pace of recovery.
  • Syngenta's preemption MSJ (filed Feb 2026) — post-Durnell, a grant could gut failure-to-warn theories for opt-out and non-eligible inventory.
  • Opt-out firm inventory — the MSA excludes clients of specific opt-out firms; watch how that inventory is priced, tried, or folded back in.
  • Philadelphia CCP bellwethers — Mertens settled on the eve of trial; the next bellwether was set for 2026-04-13 (outcome unverified) and 1,952 cases remain pending. A plaintiff verdict there would re-price the opt-out tail.
  • EPA regulatory track — Syngenta is ending global paraquat production by end of June 2026 while advocates press for a ban; regulatory action affects both future claims and public narrative.
